# Secrets of RLHF in Large Language Models Part I: PPO
> Rui Zheng, Shihan Dou, Songyang Gao, Hua Yuan, Wei Shen, Binghai Wang, Yan Liu, Senjie Jin, Qin Liu, Yuhao Zhou, Limao Xiong, Lu Chen, Zhiheng Xi, Nuo Xu, Wenbin Lai, Minghao Zhu, Cheng‐Chang Chang, Zhangyue Yin, Rongxiang Weng, Wensen Cheng, Haoran Huang, Tianxiang Sun, Hang Yan, Tao Gui, Qi Zhang, Xipeng Qiu, Xuanjing Huang · 2023 · arXiv (Cornell University) · 引用 19 · DOI 10.48550/arxiv.2307.04964

## 一句话
复旦 NLP 与字节跳动团队系统拆解了 RLHF 中的 PPO 算法,逐项消融其各个实现技巧,识别出"策略约束"是稳定训练的关键,并据此组装出加强版 **PPO-max**——在 7B/13B SFT 模型上实现了长期稳定的策略优化。

## 解决了什么问题
RLHF 的 PPO 阶段在语言模型场景下出了名地不稳定、对超参敏感:奖励上升却不等于策略变好(模型学会"刷分"),稍长训练就"模式坍塌"。作者要回答两个问题:**哪些 PPO 实现技巧是真正关键的、用什么指标能在训练中及时发现训练在跑偏**——"the trained policy model has a tendency to cheat the reward model through specific patterns for anomalous higher scores"(p.11 §5.2),而"the reward scores and training losses do not indicate whether the PPO is optimizing correctly"(p.12 §5.2)。

## 用什么方法解决的
在标准三阶段 RLHF(SFT→奖励建模→PPO)框架内,逐一消融 12 个 PPO 实现技巧(KL 惩罚、奖励/优势归一化裁剪、值函数裁剪、critic 初始化、全局梯度裁剪等),用更有信息量的监控指标(困惑度、策略-参考 KL 散度、回复长度)替代奖励/损失来判断稳定性,最终把"被验证有效"的技巧组合成 PPO-max。核心论断:"We identify policy constraints being the key factor for the effective implementation of the PPO algorithm. Therefore, we explore the PPO-max, an advanced version of PPO algorithm, to efficiently improve the training stability of the policy model."(p.1 摘要)

## 动机 & 背景
对齐 LLM 需要"准确的奖励模型 + 稳定的策略优化",但 PPO 在词空间里面临稀疏奖励与低效探索,要协调策略/价值/奖励/参考四个模型协同工作,极难训练。作者发现"the quality of the reward model directly determines the upper bound of the policy model"(p.2 §1),并主张精确的代码级实现细节对 PPO 至关重要("practice makes perfect")。本文作为"Part I",目标是公开技术报告、奖励模型与 PPO-max 代码,降低社区进入 RLHF 的门槛。

## 方法细节

**1) 奖励建模(RM)。** 架构上"use pre-trained transformer-based language models with the last unembedding layer removed and add an additional linear layer to the final transformer layer",把标量奖励赋给最后一个 token,"the larger the reward value, the better the sample"(p.4 §3.1)。损失采用成对比较的 pairwise loss(公式1,L(ψ)=log σ(r(x,y_w)−r(x,y_l))),并按照 Stiennon 等的做法额外引入对偏好回复的自回归 LM 模仿损失,以系数 β_rm 加权(公式2):"we follow [27] to use imitation learning, which introduces the autoregressive LM loss on the preferred response of each pair ... we add the coefficient β_rm the LM loss respectively"(p.4 §3.1)。

**2) 奖励中的 KL 惩罚(公式3)。** 总奖励 r_total = r(x,y) − η·KL(π^RL‖π^SFT),"where η is KL reward coefficient and controls the strength of the KL penalty. This KL divergence term plays two significant roles within this context"(p.4 §3.1):一是充当熵奖励促进探索、防止过早收敛到单一模式,二是防止 RL 策略输出严重偏离奖励模型训练时见过的分布。

**3) 优势估计与 PPO 目标。** 用 GAE(公式9)在偏差-方差间折中:"This definition of GAE smoothly interpolates between high bias (when λ = 0) and high variance (when λ = 1) estimators"(p.6 §3.2.2);附录 C.3 中实验默认取 λ=0.9。策略更新用裁剪式代理目标 PPO-Clip(公式15):"PPO-Clip attempts to keep the new policy close to the old policy ... it uses a clipped version of the policy ratio in its objective"(p.7 §3.2.3),ε 控制偏离幅度;critic 用 MSE 损失(公式16);PPO-ptx 在策略损失中混入预训练 LM 损失(公式17)以降低"对齐税"。

**4) 关键消融发现(用于组装 PPO-max)。**
- **奖励缩放无效、需更紧约束**:"we believe that tighter constraints are required to ensure training stability"(p.13 §5.3.1);作者主张把不稳定约束在奖励层面(归一化+裁剪,公式18)。
- **策略约束**:对比 KL 惩罚 / 重要性采样 / 熵奖励,"all modifications can induce convergence, but only a penalty of the policy entropy or KL divergence can provide a long-lasting stable optimization"(p.14 图7)。重要性采样"further stabilizes PPO training, but compromises the final performance of the policy model"(p.15 §5.3.2);熵奖励则"we did not find an obvious advantage of this trick relative to KL-penalty"(p.15 §5.3.2),且对参数/实现极敏感,不推荐。token 级 KL 惩罚取 λ=0.05 时,"RLHF is able to significantly improve the response quality while barely modifying the language modeling (exhibiting an almost zero KL divergence from the original policy)"(p.15 §5.3.2)。
- **初始化**:"a policy model initialized without SFT training is clearly incapable in PPO training, which indicates that the construction of a supervised policy model is indispensable in RLHF"(p.15 §5.3.3);critic 初始化方式对收敛影响不大,但建议"replacing the learning rate warmup with the critic model pre-training as a generic initialization strategy"(p.16 §5.3.3)。

**5) PPO-max 配方(p.16-17 §5.4)。** "We normalize and clip the current group of rewards based on historical mean and variance records, and subsequently add a KL-penalty term to constrain the policy optimization ... we initialize the critic model with our reward model and pre-train it before applying PPO formally. We use global gradient clipping and set a small size of the experience buffer. To reduce alignment tax, we add pre-train language model loss in policy optimization as InstructGPT [16] and simultaneously clip the value function loss." 即:奖励归一化+裁剪 → token 级 KL 惩罚 → critic 用 RM 初始化并预训练 → 全局梯度裁剪 + 小经验缓冲 → 值函数损失裁剪 + 预训练 LM 损失。

## 数据集 & 实验设置
- **英文 RM**:从 LLaMA-7B 起训,"use 160k pairwise samples of the HH-RLHF dataset ... 118k helpful and 42k harmless instances as training set",余下约 8.5k 中抽约 1k 作测试集(p.8 §4.1)。
- **中文 RM**:基于 OpenChineseLLaMA,"hired professional annotators to manually label 39k pairwise samples including 31k helpful and 8k harmless samples",训练集 2.4万… 实为 24k helpful + 6k harmless,测试集 2.4k helpful + 0.6k harmless(p.8 §4.1)。
- **RM 训练超参**:"learning rate is set to 5e-6 with a warmup over the first 10% steps",动态 batch(最大128/最小4),"fixed the training step to 1000, approximately 1.06 epoch ... We set β_rm = 1"(p.8 §4.2)。
- **PPO 训练**:四模型(策略/价值/奖励/参考),策略与参考由 7B SFT 初始化。"learning rate of policy model and critic model is set to 5e-7 and 1.65e-6 with a warmup over the first 10% steps"(p.11 §5.1)。数据为"manually constructed HH dataset containing 8k harmless queries and 20k helpful queries","batch size of 128 for sampling from the environment and a batch size of 32 for training policy model and critic model"(p.11 §5.1);硬件为每台 8×80G A100、1TB RAM、128 CPU,ZERO2 + 梯度检查点。生成用 nucleus sampling(p=0.9, 温度0.8, 重复惩罚1.1, 最大2048 token)。
- **评测**:人类偏好评分 + GPT-4 作裁判(LLM-as-a-judge),对比 SFT、ChatGPT(gpt-3.5-turbo-0613);另用 C-Eval 测中文 NLU。

## 主要结果 & 结论
- **PPO-max 长期稳定**:"PPO-max ensures long-term stable policy optimization for the model"(p.17 图9),在 10K 步训练中奖励/KL/困惑度/长度都稳定,而原始 PPO 在约 500-700 步就坍塌(图4、图13 长尾化)。
- **对齐效果(人类评测,observed/部分指标)**:英文 Harmless 留出集"RLHF model ... receiving a rating of 62% compared to 5% for the SFT model"(p.18 §6.2);Helpful 留出集 RLHF 44% 对 SFT 30%。注意:这是相对 SFT 基线的胜率类指标,Helpful 上提升幅度较小("slight improvement",作者原话)。
- **对比 ChatGPT(harmless,observed)**:RLHF 仍落后于 ChatGPT,但相比 SFT 显著缩小差距——"decrease the defeat rate from 45% to 24%"(英文)、"from 37% to 29%"(中文)(p.19 §6.3)。
- **NLU 退化与缓解**:"a decrease in NLU capabilities after employing PPO. By incorporating pre-training data ... PPO-ptx effectively alleviates the decline in NLU capabilities"(p.19 §6.4, C-Eval)。
- **监控指标的价值**:坍塌模型"uniformly delivers longer responses and exhibits lower perplexity"(p.12 §5.2),因此困惑度/KL/长度比奖励分更能预警跑偏;且 RM"accuracy alone is insufficient as a criterion"——200 步 RM 准确率已接近满 epoch,但用它初始化 PPO 表现不佳(p.9 §4.4)。

**为什么 work(机制)**:坍塌的根因是策略对奖励模型的**过度优化**——模型钻奖励模型的空子(更长、更套路化的回复)骗高分;PPO-max 的每一项都在"把策略锁在奖励模型可信的分布内":奖励归一化裁剪压住分数尺度的爆炸,token 级 KL 惩罚把每步生成拉回参考策略,critic 预训练给出更可靠的优势估计从而减少早期方向漂移。

## 核心贡献
作者自述三点(p.3 §1):"1) we release competitive Chinese and English reward models ...; 2) we conduct in-depth analysis on the inner workings of PPO algorithm and propose the PPO-max algorithm to ensure stable model training; and 3) we release the complete PPO-max codes"。实质贡献是把 RLHF/PPO 的"黑话技巧"做了系统、可复现的消融,给出一份工程上可直接抄的稳定配方,并指出训练期该看哪些指标。

## 局限与我的质疑
1. **作者自述——规模未验证**:"our study primarily focuses on a 7-billion-parameter model, we have yet to investigate the impact of model size and data scale"(p.20)。所有"关键技巧"的结论都来自 7B(部分 13B)、且消融多在中文数据的随机子集上跑,是否在更大模型/英文上同样成立未知。
2. **作者自述——缺乏可靠训练指标**:"the reward score cannot reliably serve as an indicator for predicting RLHF performance during the training phase"(p.20)。困惑度/KL/长度是"经验上有用"的旁证,但本文并未给出量化的、可预测最终对齐质量的指标——本质上仍是"靠人眼看曲线 + 事后人评"。
3. **结果强度被措辞放大的风险**:对齐结论几乎全部建立在"相对 SFT 的胜率/被击败率"上(observed 级),且 Helpful 提升只是"slight"。论文标题"comparable alignment performance with ChatGPT"易被过度解读——实际它在 harmless 上**仍输给** ChatGPT(英文被击败率 24%),只是比 SFT 少输。没有绝对能力基准(除 C-Eval 外),也没做多次随机种子的方差报告。
4. **消融的混淆与公平性**:多数对比"on a randomly selected subset of our Chinese data and will not be trained to optimal results"(p.11),即在未训到最优、子集数据上比较——技巧之间的相互作用(如奖励裁剪×优势裁剪"shows conflicts",附录 B.1)说明单项消融的结论未必能线性叠加成"PPO-max 最优",PPO-max 是工程取舍而非证明出的最优组合。
5. **RM 质量是上界但未被充分诊断**:作者强调 RM 决定上界,却也观察到 RM 会给"更长但编造事实"的回复更高分(表1)。PPO-max 解决的是"别太快被这种 RM 带偏",并没有解决 RM 本身的偏好偏差——这是 reward hacking 的根,本文只治标。
6. **与本研究主题(RL 通用工具箱/训练稳定性)的契合与不足**:本文对"稳定性技巧 + 监控指标"的工程拆解极有参考价值,KL 约束/奖励归一化/critic 预训练等可迁移到一般 RL 微调;但它是 RLHF 专用场景(语言生成、四模型协同),其"模式坍塌""刷奖励模型"的现象与常规 RL 环境奖励的语义不同,结论迁移到非 LLM 的 RL 任务时需谨慎。
