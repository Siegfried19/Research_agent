# What Matters In On-Policy Reinforcement Learning? A Large-Scale Empirical Study
> Marcin Andrychowicz, Anton Raichuk, Piotr Stańczyk, Manu Orsini, Sertan Girgin, Raphaël Marinier, Léonard Hussenot, Matthieu Geist, Olivier Pietquin, Marcin Michalski, Sylvain Gelly, Olivier Bachem · 2020 · arXiv (Cornell University) · 引用 278 · DOI 10.48550/arxiv.2006.05990

## 一句话
把 on-policy 连续控制 RL 里 50 多个平时没人细说的实现/超参"选择"放进一个统一框架，用超过 25 万个 agent 的大规模实验逐一测出"到底哪些选择重要"，并给出可直接照搬的训练建议。

## 解决了什么问题
RL 算法概念上很简单，但 SOTA 实现里塞满了大量低层和高层设计决策（损失函数细节、网络初始化、归一化、技巧），这些"选择"在论文里几乎不讨论，导致"论文描述"和"真实代码"脱节，难以归因进展。作者要做的就是：在受控条件下系统量化这些选择对最终性能的影响，把"经验/口口相传"变成有实验支撑的建议。

## 用什么方法解决的
把所有选择实现成同一个 on-policy agent 的可配置开关（"we (1) implement >50 choices in a unified on-policy algorithm implementation, (2) conducted a large-scale (more than 250'000 agents trained) experimental study"，p.1），再分主题组扫描、统计哪些选择值能稳定进入高性能配置。

---

## 动机 & 背景
作者指出 RL 的可复现性问题：不同代码库实现差异巨大，使得"算法改进"和"实现技巧改进"混在一起无法区分（§1、§4 引用 Engstrom et al.、Henderson et al.、Islam et al.）。本文不是提新算法，而是把工程师们隐式知道、却没系统验证的实现细节摊开来测。基线选 PPO，因为它"probably the most commonly used on-policy RL algorithm at moment"（p.2，§2 "Unified on-policy learning algorithm" 第 3 点：原文 "We chose PPO because it is probably the most commonly used on-policy RL algorithm at moment"）。

## 方法细节
- **统一 agent**："we implemented a single, unified on-policy agent and corresponding training protocol starting from the SEED RL code base"（p.2）。每个选择有编号（C1…C68）和固定名字，便于在附录 B 查到定义。作者验证了：当所有选择取 OpenAI baselines 的 PPO 默认值时，能复现 PPO 论文报告的性能（p.2，§2 第 3 点："We verified that when all choices are selected as in the PPO implementation from OpenAI baselines, we obtain similar performance as reported in the PPO paper"）。
- **环境**：5 个 OpenAI Gym/MuJoCo 连续控制任务——"Hopper-v1, Walker2d-v1, HalfCheetah-v1, Ant-v1, and Humanoid-v1"（p.2）；实验用 MuJoCo 2.0（p.2 脚注 1：原文 "We used Mujoco 2.0 in our experiments"，并指出 MuJoCo 版本会轻微影响部分环境行为）。
- **PPO 损失（基线损失）**："This loss encourages the policy to take actions which are better than average (have positive advantage) while clipping discourages bigger changes to the policy"（p.12 §B.3），即对重要性比 π/μ 做裁剪。
- **优势估计**：默认用 GAE，"Generalized Advantage Estimator, GAE(λ) [9] is a method that combines multi-step returns"（p.12 §B.2），λ 控制偏差-方差权衡。
- **实验设计**：分 8 个主题组（"We run experiments for eight thematic groups"，p.3）：策略损失、网络结构、归一化与裁剪、优势估计、训练设置、时间步处理、优化器、正则化。每组内随机采样组内选择、其余固定为接近 PPOv2 的强基线配置（p.3："All other settings … are set to settings of a competitive base configuration … that is close to the default PPOv2 configuration scaled up to 256 parallel environments"；该基线默认值表见附录 C 的 Table 2，已逐项核对：num_envs=256、iteration_size=2048、num_epochs=10、batch_size=64、γ=0.99、tanh 激活、Initializer=Orthogonal with gain 1.41、Adam lr=3e-4、GAE λ=0.95 等）。**注意**：Adam learning rate（C24）**不属于"其余固定"项**——作者刻意把它加入**所有**主题组一起随机采样，因为怀疑它会和很多其他选择交互（p.3，§"Basic experimental design"：原文 "We also include Adam learning rate (C24) in all of the groups as we suspect that it may interact with many other choices"）。
- **两种分析角度**："条件 95 分位数"（给定某选择值时，对性能分布取 95 分位，对应"随机搜索约 20 次能稳健达到的水平"）和"top 5% 配置中该选择值的分布"——若某值在顶配里被过度代表，说明它对拿到好性能很关键（"We report the 95th percentile of the performance as well as a confidence interval based on a binomial approximation"，p.3）。

## 数据集 & 实验设置
性能度量很严谨："we train 3 models with independent random seeds where each model is trained for one million (Hopper, HalfCheetah, Walker2d) or two million (Ant, Humanoid) environment steps"（p.3）。每 10 万步冻结策略评估："We evaluate trained policies every hundred thousand steps by freezing the policy and computing the average undiscounted episode return of 100 episodes"（p.3）；单种子分数取学习曲线下面积（奖励学得快的 agent），配置最终分取 3 个种子的**中位数**以削弱种子噪声。

## 主要结果 & 结论
> ⚠️ 以下多数结论是**在这 5 个 MuJoCo 任务上观察到**的，且作者明确强调研究目标不是"宣布某个损失普遍最好"。措辞按原文证据强度区分。

- **最意外发现（强主张）——策略初始化**："the policy initialization scheme significantly influences the performance while it is rarely even mentioned in RL publications. In particular, we have found that initializing the network so that the initial action distribution has zero mean, a rather low standard deviation and is independent of the observation significantly improves the training speed"（p.2）。具体地，仅把策略 MLP 最后一层用更小权重初始化，"this alone boosts the performance on Humanoid by 66%"（p.5，单环境观察）；性能对初始动作标准差很敏感，"0.5 performing best on all environments except Hopper where higher values perform better"（p.5）。
- **策略损失（有系统实验支撑）**："We observe that PPO performs better than the other losses on 4 out of 5 environments and is one of the top performing losses on HalfCheetah."（p.4）。机制解释：PG 和 V-trace 在所有任务上更差，"This is likely caused by their inability to handle data that become off-policy in one iteration"（p.4）——作者据此指出信赖域式优化（防止策略偏离行为策略太远）对样本效率关键（p.4，原文 "these results show that trust-region optimization (preventing the current policy from diverging too much from the behavioral one) … is crucial for good sample complexity"）。
- **网络结构**：策略与价值"Separate value and policy networks (C47) appear to lead to better performance on four out of five environments"（p.5）；激活函数"tanh activations perform best and relu worst"（p.5）。
- **归一化（强弱分环境）**："Input normalization (C64) is crucial for good performance on all environments apart from Hopper"（p.6）；价值函数归一化影响很强但**方向因环境而异**："it is crucial for good performance on HalfCheetah and Humanoid, helps slightly on Hopper and Ant and significantly hurts the performance on Walker2d"（p.6）——这是少见的"同一选择在不同任务上利害相反"的证据。
- **优势估计**："GAE and V-trace appear to perform better than N-step returns"（p.6），二者之间无显著差异；"the Huber loss (C11) performed worse than MSE in all environments"（p.6），PPO 式价值损失裁剪也有害。
- **训练设置**："going over the experience multiple times appears to be crucial for good sample complexity"（p.7）；反直觉地，"As we increase the number of parallel environments (C1), performance decreases sharply on some of the environments"（p.7，归因于经验片段变短、价值自举更早）。
- **时间步**："the performance depends heavily on the discount factor γ … with γ = 0.99 performing reasonably well in all environments. Skipping every other frame (C21) improves the performance on 2 out of 5 environments"（p.7）。
- **优化器**："the learning rate influences the performance very strongly … with the default value of 0.0003 for Adam (C24) performing well on all tasks"（p.7）；学习率线性衰减到 0 "increases the performance on 4 out of 5 tasks but the gains are very small apart from Ant, where it leads to 15% higher scores"（p.8）。
- **正则化（基本无用 + 机制解释）**："We do not find evidence that any of the investigated regularizers helps significantly on our environments with the exception of HalfCheetah on which all constraints (especially the entropy constraint) help"（p.8）。更深一层：收益"largely independent on the constraint threshold … the effect is caused by the initial high strength of the penalty (before it gets adjusted) and not by the desired constraint"（p.8）——作者推测 PPO 损失已强制信赖域、谨慎的策略初始化已保证探索，所以熵奖励/KL 约束变多余。

## 核心贡献
1. 构建统一可配置 on-policy 框架（>50 选择），跑超过 25 万 agent 的大规模受控实证（p.1，强主张）。
2. 把"策略初始化"这一被忽视的因素提为显著影响项，并给出具体可操作做法（p.2、p.5）。
3. 给每个主题组留下**可直接照搬的工程建议**（均有实验支撑）：
   - 策略损失："Use the PPO policy loss. Start with the clipping threshold set to 0.25 but also try lower and higher values if possible."（p.4）
   - 初始化："Initialize the last policy layer with 100× smaller weights. Use softplus to transform network output into action standard deviation and add a (negative) offset to its input to decrease the initial standard deviation of actions."（p.5）
   - 归一化："Always use observation normalization and check if value function normalization improves performance. Gradient clipping might slightly help but is of secondary importance."（p.6）
   - 优势估计："Use GAE with λ = 0.9 but neither Huber loss nor PPO-style value loss clipping."（p.6）
   - 训练设置："Go over experience multiple times. Shuffle individual transitions before assigning them to minibatches and recompute advantages once per data pass"（p.7）
   - 时间步："Discount factor γ is one of the most important hyperparameters and should be tuned per environment (start with γ = 0.99)."（p.7）
   - 优化器：用带动量的 Adam，"a tuned learning rate (0.0003 is a safe bet)"（p.8）。

## 局限与我的质疑
1. **泛化范围窄（作者部分自述）**：全部结论只在 5 个 MuJoCo 连续控制任务、且都以 PPO 为基线配置得出。作者自己强调研究目标"is **not** to provide a general statement that one of the losses is better than the others"（p.4，§3.1 study description 原文），但很多读者会把"PPO 在 4/5 环境更好""tanh 最好""λ=0.9"当成普适定律——这是对结论的过度解读风险。离散动作、稀疏奖励、像素输入、真实机器人等场景完全未覆盖。
2. **"X 个环境里有 Y 个"= 强弱混杂的弱证据**：大量关键结论是 observed 级（如隔帧跳过只在 2/5、线性衰减只在 Ant 上有意义提升、价值归一化在 Walker2d 上反而显著有害）。这类"多数环境有效但有反例"的结果，恰恰说明这些选择**需要按任务实测**而非照搬建议；论文给的统一 recommendation 在一定程度上掩盖了这种任务依赖性。
3. **"条件 95 分位数 + top 5%"度量本身偏向"调得好的上限"**：它衡量的是"若把组内其他选择也一起随机搜约 20 次能达到的稳健好成绩"，反映的是潜力上限而非默认表现。对一个不做超参搜索、只想拿默认值跑的实践者，这套统计可能高估了某选择的实际收益；不同选择间的强交互（作者自己承认 lr×minibatch 等会相互作用，也正因如此才把 Adam lr 放进所有组一起采样）也使"单因素"结论需谨慎。
4. **缺学习曲线/方差的细粒度披露**：性能压缩成"曲线下面积的种子中位数"，对最终收敛性能、训练稳定性、失败率（论文提到随机配置常常完全学不动）等区分不足；附录虽给了大量分位数表与图，但很多关键比较仍是柱状图肉眼读数，难以判断差异显著性。
5. **与本研究主题（RL 训练交互式 agent / 通用工具箱）的契合度（总结者注）**：本文是连续控制 locomotion 基准上的"实现细节调参指南"，对"训练能与环境/人交互的数字人"这类更复杂、可能含离散决策或多模态输入的任务，其具体数值建议（如初始 std=0.5、γ=0.99、lr=3e-4）未必直接适用——可借鉴的是**方法论**（把实现选择当一等公民系统消融）和**初始化重要性**这一定性洞见，而非照搬超参。
